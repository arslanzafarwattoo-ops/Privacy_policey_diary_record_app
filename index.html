Privacy Commitment: Wattoo Dairy is designed offline-first. Your livestock data, daily milk logs, worker records, and financial entries belong entirely to you and are stored locally on your device. We do not sell, rent, track, or share your farm data with third-party advertisers or data brokers.

1. Introduction & Overview

This Privacy Policy describes how Wattoo Dairy ("the Application", "we", "us", or "our") manages, processes, and protects your information. The app is built specifically for dairy farm owners, livestock breeders, and agricultural workers to track herd animals, morning/evening milk production, farm staff records, feed ration experiments, and veterinary vaccination reminders.

By downloading or using Wattoo Dairy, you agree to the terms outlined in this policy.

2. Information Collection and Storage

Wattoo Dairy operates primarily as a local offline database using Android's native SQLite Room architecture. The types of data entered into the application include:

🐾 Livestock & Animals

Animal tag number, code, breed, species, gender, date of birth / farm arrival date, health status, fattening tag, and purchase prices.

🥛 Milk Production

Daily morning (day) and evening (night) milk harvest in liters, price per liter, and total daily milk value.

💰 Financial Records

Farm expenses (feed, wanda, silage, veterinary, labor, electricity) and farm income (milk sales, animal sales, manure sales).

👥 Farm Workers

Worker names, phone numbers, job roles, monthly salaries, advances given, and payment dates.

🧪 Feed Experiments

Feed ration formulas (Wanda, Silage, Toxin Binder, Mitha Soda), cost calculations, weight gain logs, and milk yield impact.

💉 Health & Vaccines

Vaccination reminders (FMD, HS, BQ, Deworming), dosage, scheduled dates, and vet contact names.

All the above records are stored locally on your physical device in sandboxed private storage. We do not transmit or host your operational farm records on external proprietary servers.

3. Device Permissions Disclosed

Wattoo Dairy follows the principle of least privilege and requests only the minimal permissions required for core farm management features:

Permission Classification Purpose in Application
POST_NOTIFICATIONS
android.permission.POST_NOTIFICATIONS		 Runtime Permission Required on Android 13+ to send scheduled local push notifications for upcoming animal vaccinations (e.g., FMD, HS, Anthrax, Deworming) scheduled by the user. Notifications are generated locally by Android AlarmManager.
VIBRATE
android.permission.VIBRATE		 Install-time Normal Provides haptic vibration feedback for timely alarm notifications and confirmation dialogs.
Photo Selection (Zero-Permission) Photo Picker API When adding optional photos of animals or workers, the app uses Android's privacy-preserving Photo Picker (PickVisualMedia). The app does not request broad storage permissions (READ_EXTERNAL_STORAGE or READ_MEDIA_IMAGES).

4. PDF Report Generation & Printing

Wattoo Dairy includes offline document generation for Monthly Milk Ledgers and Day & Night production reports. When you select "Print / Save PDF":

  • The PDF is created locally on your device via Android's native PdfDocument and PrintManager framework.
  • No document contents or financial records are uploaded to cloud servers during PDF creation or printing.
  • Exported PDF files are saved to your device's local application storage and can be shared only upon your explicit action via the Android system share sheet.

5. Third-Party Services and Analytics

Wattoo Dairy is built without third-party advertising networks, tracking beacons, or cross-app analytics trackers:

  • No Ad Networks: We do not integrate AdMob, Unity Ads, Facebook Audience Network, or any other advertising SDK.
  • No User Profiling: We do not collect device advertising IDs (AAID), hardware identifiers, or location coordinates.
  • No Data Brokering: We never sell, lease, or monetize user data.

6. Data Retention, Backup, and Deletion Rights

You have complete control over your farm data at all times:

  • Data Export: You can export all your dairy farm records anytime as a CSV spreadsheet or JSON backup directly through the Settings & Reports screen.
  • Permanent Deletion: You can permanently wipe all records by selecting "Clear All Farm Data" inside the Settings tab, or by clearing the app data via Android Settings, or by uninstalling the application.
  • Data Portability: JSON backups can be copied or restored to another device without requiring an online account.

7. Children's Privacy (COPPA Compliance)

Wattoo Dairy is a professional agricultural utility intended for farmers, livestock managers, and adults. The application is not directed at children under the age of 13 (or under the age of 16 in the European Union). We do not knowingly solicit or collect personal information from children.

8. Security Safeguards

Your data is secured through Android's application sandboxing architecture, ensuring that other apps installed on your device cannot access your farm records. We encourage users to maintain a screen lock (PIN, password, or biometric) on their device to protect physical access to their dairy records.
